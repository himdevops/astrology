Rules Reference — Moon Market Analysis ▼
Rule 1: Tithi (Moon Phase) ▼
RangeSignal
Krishna Ekadashi (K11) to Shukla Panchami (S5)Bullish
Shukla Shashthi (S6) to Krishna Dashami (K10)Bearish
Tithi is the lunar day. The market is considered bullish from K11 through Amavasya to S5, and bearish from S6 through Purnima to K10.

Rule 5: Shadashtak (6/8 Relationship) ▼
PairSignal

Exception: Saturn-Moon Dwidamsha ▼
When Saturn and Moon are in a 2/12 relationship, the signal is always Bullish regardless of the directional pair. This overrides the standard Dwidamsha rule for this specific combination.
