These three nakshatras are highlighted on the grid: Green = Janma, Orange = Karma, Red = Vinasha.

10. Surya Bindu — Sun's Sensitive Points (सूर्य बिन्दु) ▼
Bindu ComponentDerived FromSBC CellVedha Effect
Sun's Nakshatra सूर्य नक्षत्रNatal Sun's nakshatra positionThe nakshatra cell where natal Sun is placed on the borderTransit vedha here affects vitality, self-confidence, authority
Sun's Rashi सूर्य राशिNatal Sun's zodiac signThe rashi cell on the inner border matching Sun's signTransit vedha here affects social status, government matters
Surya Bindu (सूर्य बिन्दु) represents the Sun's sensitive points on the SBC grid. These are derived from the natal Sun's position — its nakshatra and rashi. When transit planets (especially malefics like Saturn, Rahu, Mars) create vedha on Surya Bindu cells, the native faces challenges related to authority, career, health (vitality), government dealings, and father-related matters.
Benefic vedha (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury) on Surya Bindu brings promotions, recognition, improved health, and government favor.
Malefic vedha (Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Ketu) on Surya Bindu causes authority conflicts, demotion, health issues, and government troubles.

Bindu ComponentDerived FromSBC CellVedha Effect

Moon's Rashi चन्द्र राशिNatal Moon's zodiac sign (Janma Rashi)The rashi cell on the inner border matching Moon's signVedha here affects emotional state, domestic life, mother
Birth Tithi Group जन्म तिथिTithi at birth (Nanda/Bhadra/Jaya/Rikta/Poorna)The tithi cell in the center of the gridVedha here affects timing of events, auspiciousness
Birth Vara जन्म वारWeekday of birthThe vara cell in the center of the gridVedha here affects daily rhythms, work patterns
Janma Akshar जन्म अक्षरFirst sound/letter of the native's name (from Moon's nakshatra pada)The vowel/pada cell on the gridVedha here directly targets the native's identity
Chandra Bindu (चन्द्र बिन्दु) is the PRIMARY reference in SBC analysis. It represents all the sensitive points derived from the natal Moon. The Moon's nakshatra (Janma Nakshatra) is the single most important cell in the entire SBC for any native.
Key principle: Any transit planet that vedhas even ONE Chandra Bindu cell creates an effect on the native. The more Chandra Bindu cells that are vedha'd simultaneously, the stronger the effect.
Benefic vedha on Chandra Bindu = mental peace, good health, prosperity, auspicious events.
Malefic vedha on Chandra Bindu = mental unrest, health issues, obstacles, inauspicious events.
Note: Chandra Bindu has more components than Surya Bindu because the Moon governs the mind (manas), which is central to all human experience in Vedic astrology.
12. Asta Disha — Combust Directions (अस्त दिशा) ▼
PlanetDirection RuledCombustion ThresholdWhen Combust
Sun सूर्यEast (पूर्व)—Sun is never combust (it causes combustion)
Moon चन्द्रNorthwest (वायव्य)Within 12° of SunNW direction blocked — avoid travel/new ventures in NW
Mars मंगलSouth (दक्षिण)Within 17° of SunSouth direction blocked — avoid southward travel
Mercury बुधNorth (उत्तर)Within 14° (12° if Rx)North direction blocked — avoid northward travel
Jupiter गुरुNortheast (ईशान)Within 11° of SunNE direction blocked — avoid pilgrimages/education in NE
Venus शुक्रSoutheast (आग्नेय)Within 10° (8° if Rx)SE direction blocked — avoid SE travel/relationships
Saturn शनिWest (पश्चिम)Within 15° of SunWest direction blocked — avoid westward travel
Rahu राहुSouthwest (नैऋत्य)—Rahu/Ketu are shadow planets — not combust by Sun
Ketu केतु——Rahu/Ketu are shadow planets — not combust by Sun
Asta Disha (अस्त दिशा) means "set/blocked direction." When a planet is combust (asta) — too close to the Sun — the direction it rules becomes inauspicious. The native should not travel in that direction, nor start new ventures associated with that direction.
Combustion (Asta): A planet is combust when it is within the specified degree range of the Sun in longitude. The planet's rays are overpowered by the Sun, making it powerless.
Retrograde exception: Mercury and Venus have slightly tighter combustion ranges when retrograde because they are closer to Earth and appear brighter.
Practical use: Before travel or starting a new venture, check Asta Disha. If the direction you need to travel is blocked by a combust planet, either wait until the planet moves away from the Sun, or choose an alternate route/timing.
